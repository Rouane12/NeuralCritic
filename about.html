OUR MISSION

Gaming editorial without the noise.

Neural Critic is an independent gaming publication focused on thoughtful features, useful reviews, guides, and stories built for players who value clarity over clutter.

What we publish

Features that examine design, reviews that explain the experience behind the score, and guides that respect the player’s time.

Who runs it

Neural Critic is created and edited by Rouane Mounssif under the NeuralHustle brand.
