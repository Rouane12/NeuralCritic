# Neural Critic — X Publishing Templates

Use these as starting points. Adjust the hook to the actual story; do not force every post into identical wording.

## Tracking rule

Every article link distributed on X should use:

- `utm_source=x`
- `utm_medium=social`
- `utm_campaign=<story-or-campaign-slug>`
- `utm_content=<placement>`

Recommended placement values:
- `original_post`
- `reply`
- `thread`
- `pinned_post`

## Breaking news

> BREAKING: [one-sentence development].
>
> [Most important confirmed detail].
>
> [Second useful detail / what remains unknown].
>
> Full story ↓
> [tracked URL]

Rules:
- Use BREAKING only for genuinely time-sensitive developments.
- Attribute claims clearly when the news comes from a publisher, studio, platform, filing, interview, or report.
- Do not turn rumors into confirmed language.

Rules:

- One relevant link is enough; do not repeat the same story across many replies.

## Posting guardrails

- Lead with value, not “new article.”
- Avoid bare-link posts.
- Prefer official screenshots/key art or properly credited/licensed editorial media.
- Do not overuse hashtags; use none unless they materially aid discovery.
- Avoid tagging accounts purely to chase reach.
- Do not spam replies under unrelated trending posts.
- Keep NeuralCritic.net as the canonical/full destination.
- Use X Articles selectively; do not duplicate full Neural Critic stories by default.
- When a major story is active, one strong original post plus a few genuinely relevant replies is better than repeated promotional posts.

## Default workflow for every published story

1. Publish the canonical Neural Critic story.
2. Create a campaign slug and tracked `original_post` URL.
3. Publish one strong native X post using the appropriate template.
4. Monitor `Official Gaming Sources`, `Gaming News Desk`, and `Neural Critic Watchlist` for natural reply opportunities.
5. If a relevant conversation appears, use a separate `utm_content=reply` link.
6. Check post/reply performance after roughly 1 hour when useful, then at 24 hours and 7 days for meaningful campaigns.
7. Optimize future copy based on qualified website traffic, not impressions alone.
