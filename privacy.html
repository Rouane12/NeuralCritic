PRIVACY · READER CONTROL

Privacy at Neural Critic

Neural Critic uses the minimum data needed to run reader features and, only when allowed, understand how the publication is being used.

Essential site data

The site may store local preferences such as light or dark mode and your analytics choice. Reader accounts, community features, and newsletter subscriptions use Neural Critic's Supabase backend when you choose to use those features.

Optional analytics

When Google Analytics is enabled for Neural Critic, it only loads after a reader allows analytics. Measurement is intended for publication-level signals such as page and article views, reading depth, searches, newsletter signup success, shares, follows, likes, and outbound-link domains.

Neural Critic's analytics code is designed not to send email addresses, comment text, account names, search text, or other reader-entered content to Google Analytics. Advertising storage and personalization signals remain disabled.

Publication services

  • Supabase supports publishing, reader accounts, community features, and newsletter data.
  • Google Analytics is an optional measurement service and is not loaded until analytics consent is granted and a Neural Critic GA4 property is configured.
  • GitHub Pages serves the public Neural Critic website and static assets.

Your controls

You can change the analytics preference on this page at any time. Choosing Essential Only prevents Neural Critic's analytics module from loading the Google Analytics tag on future page loads.
