# Neural Critic — X / Social Distribution Playbook

## Goal
Turn X into a measurable traffic channel for Neural Critic without turning the account into a link-spam feed.

Core funnel:

Gaming conversation → Neural Critic post/reply → profile visit → website click → article reader

## Posting model

Do not post bare links. Every article post should provide a useful hook and enough information to stand on its own.

Example structure:

> **The Witcher 3: Songs of the Past just got its biggest gameplay reveal yet.**
>
> 12 minutes of footage showed:
>
> • the new region
> • new monsters
> • quests and characters
> • Geralt's new weapon
>
> Here's everything CDPR revealed ↓
> [tracked Neural Critic URL]

Use a strong native image when appropriate.

## Reply strategy

Premium reply prioritization should be used around relevant gaming conversations, not as spam.

Good reply pattern:
1. Add an actual observation or useful context.
2. Keep the reply readable without requiring a click.
3. Link a Neural Critic story only when it directly adds value.

Avoid:

> Check our article! [link]

Prefer:

> The interesting part here is how the new footage expands exploration rather than simply adding another quest chain. The region looks substantially more open than the original teaser suggested.
>
> We broke down the new footage here: [tracked Neural Critic URL]

## UTM standard

All links manually distributed through X should use:

- `utm_source=x`
- `utm_medium=social`
- `utm_campaign=<story-or-campaign-slug>`
- `utm_content=<placement>`

Recommended `utm_content` values:
- `original_post`
- `reply`
- `thread`
- `pinned_post`
- `profile`

Example:

`https://www.neuralcritic.net/stories/example-story/?utm_source=x&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=example_story&utm_content=reply`

Do not change the canonical URL, sitemap URL, or internal article URL to include UTMs. Tracking parameters are for outbound social distribution only.

## Measurement

Review X and GA4 together.

X-side metrics:
- impressions
- engagements
- profile visits
- link clicks

Neural Critic / GA4 metrics:
- sessions from X
- engaged sessions
- article read depth
- newsletter conversion where applicable
- commerce clicks where applicable

Optimize for qualified readers, not raw impressions.

## Operating rhythm

For each meaningful new story:
1. Publish the canonical Neural Critic article.
2. Publish one strong native X post with a tracked URL.
3. Watch relevant publisher/developer/platform conversations for natural reply opportunities.
4. Use tracked reply URLs only when the article directly supports the conversation.
5. Revisit performance after 24 hours and again after 7 days.

## Guardrails

- NeuralCritic.net remains the primary publication and canonical destination.
- Do not republish full articles verbatim as X Articles by default.
- Do not spam replies with links.
- Do not buy engagement or use fake accounts.
- Do not alter editorial recommendations because of traffic or affiliate considerations.
- Premium is a distribution tool, not an editorial signal.
